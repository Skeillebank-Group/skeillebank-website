Common headaches

Sound familiar?

These are the ones we see most.

“Reports take all morning.”

It's rarely the people. It's usually the database, and it can often be fixed without changing anything your team sees.

“The system crawls every month-end.”

Something is queuing behind something else: a slow query, a missing index, two jobs waiting on each other. We find it and move it out of the way.

“We pay for tools that don't talk to each other.”

So someone types the same order in twice. We connect them so it's entered once, or replace several with one that fits.

“Only one person understands the spreadsheet.”

Until they go on holiday. We turn it into a simple app the whole team can use, one that survives someone sorting the wrong column.

“Card fees eat into every sale.”

Every tap takes a slice. For many small businesses, bank-to-bank payments by QR code cost less.

“Are we overpaying for software?”

We compare what you pay for with what you actually use, and tell you honestly what you could drop. Sometimes the answer is nothing.

Few of them need something new to buy. Most just need someone to look.

Software rarely breaks all at once. It slows down a little every year, until month-end takes a morning.

How it works

From headache to a straight answer

  1. Tell us the headache

    Book a free 30-minute call, or email a few sentences. You don't need the technical words; tell us what it feels like.

  2. We take a look

    We ask questions, look at what you use today, and follow the time and money to where they're going.

  3. You get a straight answer

    Fix what you have, build something small, switch to something cheaper, or leave it alone.

If it isn't worth doing, we'll say so.
